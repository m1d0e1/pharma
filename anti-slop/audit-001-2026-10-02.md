# Anti-Slop AFTER Audit 001 - POS, Receipts, Sales Returns

Date: 2026-10-02

Mode: AFTER

Scope: Windows/Tauri pharmacy-management UI covering POS + checkout, receipts, and sales returns.

Safety boundary: presentation/accessibility/responsive review only. No SQL, schema, migrations, accounting, inventory, permissions, tenant isolation, transaction, Rust/Tauri business semantics, or persistence changes are authorized by this audit.

Platform note: R-03 is applied to resizable narrow desktop windows and touch-capable Windows devices. This audit does not claim the Tauri app targets phones.

## Findings

### 1. HIGH - POS does not reflow safely at narrow window widths

- Rule: R-03 Mobile Responsiveness
- Classification: presentation-only
- Evidence: `src/app/(dashboard)/layout.tsx:621-623` constrains the dashboard with overflow hidden; `src/app/(dashboard)/pos/page.tsx:1196-1201` keeps the POS in one horizontal flex row with an 80px fixed action rail; `src/app/(dashboard)/pos/page.tsx:1637-1639` adds a fixed 260/300px search rail.
- Reason: the two fixed side rails plus a non-wrapping center create clipping pressure instead of a deliberate narrow-window layout.
- Safe direction: introduce a small-window reflow/collapse strategy without changing POS behavior or data flow.

### 2. HIGH - POS contains undersized interactive targets

- Rule: R-03 Mobile Responsiveness
- Classification: presentation-only
- Evidence: cart quantity decrement/increment controls are `w-4 h-4` (16x16) at `src/app/(dashboard)/pos/page.tsx:1518-1540`; the cart delete action is `p-1` around a 12px icon at `1592-1602`; patient clear and "عرض الكل" controls are also very small at `1269-1272` and `1337-1343`. Receipt print/WhatsApp actions are `p-2` at `src/components/receipts/ReceiptListClient.tsx:167-170`; receipt-modal close is `p-1.5` around a 16px icon at `src/components/receipts/ReceiptDetailsModal.tsx:167-174`; return-detail close is `p-2` around a 20px icon at `src/components/returns/ReturnsClient.tsx:265-270`.
- Reason: several controls are materially below Anti-Slop's approximately 44x44 touch-target requirement.
- Safe direction: increase hit areas while preserving the existing actions and visual density.

### 3. HIGH - POS has mouse-only / incomplete keyboard paths

- Rule: R-32 Keyboard Accessibility
- Classification: presentation-only
- Evidence: quantity +/- controls are explicitly removed from the tab order with `tabIndex={-1}` at `src/app/(dashboard)/pos/page.tsx:1519` and `1535-1540`; cart rows select only through `onClick`/`onContextMenu` at `1471-1475`; F2/F9/Delete operate on `selectedRowCartId` at `573-580` and `1137-1157`; the hand-built Tab navigation at `423-487` omits several live controls including payment methods, loyalty controls, sidebar actions, and the check-number input.
- Reason: R-32 requires every interactive path to be reachable and operable from the keyboard, not only the primary input sequence.
- Safe direction: keep the existing power-user shortcuts, but make row selection, steppers, and omitted controls reachable through normal keyboard navigation.

### 4. HIGH - Loading and empty states are incomplete or not perceivable

- Rule: R-27 UI States
- Classification: presentation-only
- Evidence: route-level POS, receipts, returns, and new-return loaders are spinner-only at `src/app/(dashboard)/pos/page.tsx:1169-1174`, `src/app/(dashboard)/receipts/page.tsx:175-180`, `src/app/(dashboard)/returns/page.tsx:42-47`, and `src/app/(dashboard)/returns/new/page.tsx:42-47`; POS drug search is spinner-only and zero results become blank at `src/app/(dashboard)/pos/page.tsx:279-319`; patient search has no explicit loading/no-results state at `586-618` and `1344-1379`; `SalesReturnClient` maintains `isSearching` at `src/app/(dashboard)/returns/new/SalesReturnClient.tsx:56` and `90-124`/`202-238` but does not render a searching state.
- Reason: data views can appear blank or frozen, and spinner-only states are not sufficiently informative/perceivable.
- Safe direction: add visible status text and appropriate status semantics, plus explicit no-results states.

### 5. HIGH - Several normal-size text colors fail WCAG AA contrast

- Rule: R-25 Color Contrast
- Classification: presentation-only
- Evidence: Anti-Slop contrast checker reports `#94A3B8` (`slate-400`) on white at 2.56:1, `#F43F5E` (`rose-500`) on white at 3.67:1, and `#D97706` (`amber-600`) on white at 3.19:1; all fail the 4.5:1 requirement for normal text. Examples include POS patient balance labels at `src/app/(dashboard)/pos/page.tsx:1274-1291`, receipt empty text at `src/components/receipts/ReceiptListClient.tsx:175-178`, receipt small discount/fee text at `src/components/receipts/ReceiptDetailsModal.tsx:263-280`, and sales-return empty/placeholder text at `src/app/(dashboard)/returns/new/SalesReturnClient.tsx:412-413` and `623-625`.
- Reason: the affected text is small/normal text, so these color pairs are below R-25's hard WCAG AA threshold.
- Safe direction: strengthen foreground tones independently for light/dark themes without changing semantic status colors.

### 6. HIGH - Return-details dialog cannot be closed with Escape

- Rule: R-32 Keyboard Accessibility / R-26 Interactive Elements
- Classification: presentation-only
- Evidence: return details use a focus trap at `src/components/returns/ReturnsClient.tsx:253-270`, but `src/hooks/useDialogFocusTrap.ts:35-56` handles only Tab. The dialog itself has no Escape handler. `ReceiptDetailsModal` is a compliant reference because it binds Escape at `src/components/receipts/ReceiptDetailsModal.tsx:53-55`.
- Reason: Anti-Slop requires dialogs to be keyboard-contained and closable with Escape.
- Safe direction: add Escape close behavior to the return-details dialog without touching return data/actions.

### 7. HIGH - Sales-return search uses misleading empty-state copy

- Rule: R-27 UI States
- Classification: presentation-only
- Evidence: free-text search calls `searchRecentReturnInvoicesAction(searchTerm)` at `src/app/(dashboard)/returns/new/SalesReturnClient.tsx:92-105`, but zero results display "لا توجد فواتير مكتملة في هذا التاريخ" at `412-413`; `ReturnsClient` searches at `src/components/returns/ReturnsClient.tsx:89-94`, yet zero search matches display first-use copy "لم تقم بإنشاء أي مرتجعات بعد" at `181-189`.
- Reason: empty-state content does not describe the state the user actually caused, which makes valid searches look like missing historical data.
- Safe direction: distinguish initial-empty from search-no-results copy only.

### 8. HIGH - POS "show all patients" path has a reproducible keyboard regression

- Rule: R-26 Interactive Elements / R-32 Keyboard Accessibility
- Classification: requires functional investigation
- Evidence: focused test `src/components/__tests__/coverage-gap-pos-hotkeys.regression.tsx:539-552` expects the keyboard-focusable "عرض الكل" action to show returned patients. In the audit run it failed: `searchPatientsAction('', true)` was called but `All Patients Result` disappeared/not rendered. The likely race is between `loadAllPatients()` at `src/app/(dashboard)/pos/page.tsx:620-639` and the initial/debounced patient-search effect at `586-618`, both incrementing `patientSearchRequestRef`.
- Reason: the control has real behavior in code but the audited regression proves the result can be invalidated before the user can act on it.
- Boundary: do not fix under presentation-only authority. Reproduce/isolate the request-id race and keep/add a focused regression before the smallest functional fix.

### 9. HIGH - Sales-return global arrow navigation may hijack focused buttons/links

- Rule: R-32 Keyboard Accessibility
- Classification: requires functional investigation
- Evidence: the window-level ArrowUp/ArrowDown handler at `src/app/(dashboard)/returns/new/SalesReturnClient.tsx:160-190` excludes only INPUT/TEXTAREA/SELECT at `163-166`; focused links/buttons remain eligible, including Back at `347-350` and Submit at `573-580`.
- Reason: arrow keys pressed while a button/link has focus can change the selected invoice instead of staying scoped to the invoice list.
- Boundary: reproduce whether prepared return state can be changed/lost from focused non-form controls before editing this behavior.

### 10. MEDIUM - POS elevation is applied almost everywhere instead of marking hierarchy

- Rule: R-12 Shadow
- Classification: presentation-only
- Evidence: search card uses `shadow-xl` at `src/app/(dashboard)/pos/page.tsx:198`; left rail `shadow-xl` at `1201`; invoice header `shadow-lg` at `1264`; cart panel `shadow-xl` at `1451`; checkout CTA adds `shadow-xl shadow-emerald-500/20` at `1627`; dialogs then use `shadow-2xl`.
- Reason: when nearly every primary surface is elevated, shadow stops communicating hierarchy and becomes a default treatment.
- Safe direction: flatten ordinary work surfaces and reserve stronger elevation for overlays/high-priority transient elements.

### 11. MEDIUM - POS icon language is mixed without a consistent purpose

- Rule: R-04 Icons
- Classification: presentation-only
- Evidence: the same operational surface mixes Lucide icons with emoji for payment methods at `src/app/(dashboard)/pos/page.tsx:108-115`, patient identity at `1270`, alternatives at `1643`, while most structural actions use Lucide icons.
- Reason: mixed icon systems make the UI feel assembled from defaults rather than intentionally designed.
- Safe direction: keep semantic meaning but standardize the icon language for routine controls. Do not change domain labels or payment behavior.

### 12. LOW - Sales-return page repeats top-level page hierarchy

- Rule: R-05 Layout & Page Structure
- Classification: presentation-only
- Evidence: `src/app/(dashboard)/returns/page.tsx:67-74` renders an H1/title block and immediately mounts `ReturnsClient`, which renders another H1/title block at `src/components/returns/ReturnsClient.tsx:137-146`.
- Reason: duplicate top-level hierarchy adds visual noise without new information.
- Safe direction: keep one page-level title and let the reusable client render a subordinate heading or no duplicate heading when embedded.
